title: "Amazon Connect Customer launches the ability to interrupt an agent with an urgent contact"

date: "2026-06-18"
feed_url: "https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/recent/feed/"
---
Agents can now receive urgent calls that override normal routing, enabling them to decide whether to put the first caller on hold to pick up the callback. Custom statuses can exclude agents from regular queues while still allowing personal extension calls.
