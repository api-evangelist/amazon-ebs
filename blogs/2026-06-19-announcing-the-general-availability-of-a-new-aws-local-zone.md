AWS introduces a new Local Zone in Hanoi with support for Amazon S3 and Amazon EBS Local Snapshots. The deployment brings AWS infrastructure closer to end users in the Asia Pacific region, enabling data residency compliance and reduced latency. Supported services include EC2 instances, ECS, EKS, and Application Load Balancers.
